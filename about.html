FAQ

Questions founders usually ask

Are you open to full-time Senior Product Manager roles?

Yes. I'm looking at Senior PM and Product Lead roles at startups and scale-ups, especially in EdTech, HealthTech, consumer subscription and B2B SaaS, remote or in Delhi NCR.

Do you take freelance or fractional product work?

For the right problem, yes. I've delivered freelance product work for HobFit and Nexteir. Typical engagements are a 0-to-1 MVP, a funnel and pricing overhaul, or setting up analytics and CRM from scratch.

How technical are you?

I spent two years as a software engineer before moving to product. I write SQL and Python, read API contracts and database schemas, and work with engineers on payment architecture, event tracking and deployments.

What size of team do you work best in?

Early-stage teams where the PM owns outcomes across product, growth and revenue. At ReflexPrep I joined a 4-person company and led a 3-engineer team plus design and growth.
