Product work, measured.

What was broken, what I changed, and the number that moved. Six case studies from ReflexPrep and HobFit.

Growth · ReflexPrep

From free trial to paid plan

I instrumented the full funnel in CleverTap and GA4, fixed the onboarding and checkout drop-offs it exposed, and ran pricing, plan-order and coupon experiments. Monthly paid users went from 2.5K to 7K and leads from 80 to 350 a day.

20%→70%free-trial activation
Payments · ReflexPrep

Re-architecting the payment stack

Failed and mismatched payments were leaking revenue. I ran the RCA, led the move from Paytm to Razorpay with fallback and recovery flows, added payment-failure alerts and recovery campaigns, and redesigned the checkout page.

27%→50%payment conversion
AI · ReflexPrep

AI support agents on four channels

I built and deployed AI agents for WhatsApp, Instagram, email and the website, set up Freshdesk and a knowledge base behind them, and moved support from a 1–2 day SLA to about 30 minutes.

₹89K→₹15Kmonthly support cost (−83%)
Retention · ReflexPrep

Renewal and lifecycle journeys

I rebuilt CleverTap around the AARRR framework and created behaviour-based journeys across push, WhatsApp, email and SMS for drop-offs, milestones, expiring plans and referrals.

6%→40%renewal rate
0-to-1 · ReflexPrep

Launching the FMGE vertical

I owned the new vertical end to end: app and website, onboarding, question bank, payments, CleverTap and UTM tracking, SEO landing pages and the GTM playbook. It went live in April 2026, alongside the HAMMER and TROCAR quiz products.

700 + 400quiz participants across HAMMER and TROCAR
Conversion · HobFit

Removing friction from checkout

A women's wellness platform with 500K+ users across 5+ countries was losing buyers between the home screen and payment. I redesigned home and checkout around the drop-offs the funnel data showed, then added time-zone-based coupons, dynamic discounts and Refer, Earn & Extend features.

~2%→~10%purchase conversion
